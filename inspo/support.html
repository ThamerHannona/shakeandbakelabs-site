Common questions

How do I add a widget? Long-press your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, search INSPO, pick a size and tap Add Widget. For the Lock Screen, long-press it, tap Customize, then Lock Screen, and add INSPO.

Reminders aren't showing. Open Settings, then Notifications, then INSPO, and turn on Allow Notifications. Then set your reminder times in the app.

How do I set a wallpaper? Save a phrase as a wallpaper in INSPO, then open Photos, tap the image, tap Share, then Use as Wallpaper.

Change the language. INSPO follows your iPhone language. Go to Settings, then General, then Language & Region.
