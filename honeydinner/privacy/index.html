Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

This policy explains what Honey, Dinner? (“the app”), made by Shake and Bake Labs (“we”, “us”), collects, why, and your choices. The short version: there are no ads, no tracking, and no account to create. We use the photos, links, and voice you choose to share only to run the features you asked for.

At a glance

  • No ads. No tracking across other companies’ apps or websites. No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We never sell your data.
  • You don’t need to sign in. The app uses an anonymous identifier on your device (and, if you subscribe, an anonymous RevenueCat purchase ID) so your kitchen and subscription can work without an email or password.
  • Photos, recipe videos, and short voice commands are used only to import recipes, scan the kitchen, or cook hands-free.

Information you choose to provide

  • Recipe links and videos. When you share a public TikTok, Instagram, or similar recipe video into the app, we fetch that content to extract a recipe. That can include the link, captions, on-screen text, still images, the video itself, and the video’s audio.
  • Photos and camera images. If you scan a fridge, pantry, receipt, screenshot, or cookbook page, the app uses the camera or photos you pick.
  • Recipes, lists, and plans. Recipes you import or add, grocery lists, pantry items, meal plans, and (if you use it) a display name in your kitchen.
  • Voice commands. In hands-free cook mode, the app can listen for short commands such as “next,” “back,” and “start timer.”

How we use this information, and who helps us process it

We use the information above only to provide Honey, Dinner?: import and organize recipes, stock your kitchen, build grocery lists and meal plans, sync a shared household kitchen, speak cook-mode steps, and recognize voice commands. We do not use it for advertising.

To do that, the app sends some of this information to our servers (hosted by Supabase) and to a small number of processors:

  • OpenAI, and sometimes Google Gemini. Photos, recipe video content (including audio and images from a video you shared), and related text may be sent to OpenAI and, when that path is used, Google Gemini, so they can extract a recipe, read a fridge or receipt, write cook-mode speech, or create step pictures. We send this only to fulfill your request. We do not use this content to train our own models. Under OpenAI’s current API terms, OpenAI does not use data submitted via its API to train its models. We will review and update this policy if a provider’s terms change.
  • Apple speech recognition. Voice commands in cook mode are processed by Apple’s speech-recognition service on your device, which may send audio to Apple to turn it into text, unless Apple can complete the request on-device. We do not get a recording of your voice from this, and we do not use your commands to train models.
  • Supabase. If you create or join a household with a share code, we store that kitchen’s recipes, grocery list, pantry, meal plan, member display names, and the share code on Supabase so everyone in the kitchen stays in sync. We may also store generated step pictures (and similar generated recipe images) so they can be reused and don’t have to be created again. Scan and import images are sent through our servers to be processed; we do not keep a photo album of your kitchen for advertising or profiling.
  • RevenueCat. If you start a trial or subscribe to Honey, Dinner? Plus, Apple handles the payment. We use RevenueCat to keep track of your subscription status with an anonymous purchase identifier, not your name or email.

As with any internet request, our hosting providers may briefly see your device’s IP address to deliver the request and limit abuse. We do not build advertising profiles from it.

Permissions

The app may ask for camera, photo library, microphone, and speech-recognition access. Each is optional and used only for the feature you started (scanning, importing a photo, or hands-free cook mode). You can change these in iOS Settings at any time.

Household kitchens and deletion

A household kitchen is optional. If you create or join one, members who share the code can see the recipes, lists, and plans in that kitchen.

To stop sharing, leave the kitchen in the app. That removes you from the household. Data that remains may still be visible to people who are still in the kitchen. If you want a kitchen and its data deleted, or you want help removing information we hold, email support@shakeandbakelabs.com and we will help. Uninstalling the app removes what is stored only on your device; it does not automatically cancel a subscription or delete a shared kitchen on our servers.

Children

Honey, Dinner? is a family meal-planning app, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the age defined by local law, such as COPPA in the U.S.). Please do not add a child’s personal information to a kitchen. If you believe we have information about a child, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us and we’ll help. Because there is no account email, the most reliable way to make a request is to email us from a contact you can use for follow-up and describe the kitchen or device involved.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the effective date above.
