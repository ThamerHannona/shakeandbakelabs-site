Honey, Dinner? is an iPhone app for people who keep saving recipe videos and then… stare into the fridge. Share a TikTok or Instagram into the app, and we’ll pull out the ingredients and steps. What it does

  • Save recipes from TikTok and Instagram. Use the iOS share sheet — Honey, Dinner? reads the video and writes a proper recipe you can edit.
  • Cook hands-free. A natural voice walks you through the steps, with AI pictures for each one, so you can keep your hands on the pan.
  • Scan the fridge or a receipt. Snap a photo to stock the kitchen and check groceries off your list.
  • Plan dinner together. Share a kitchen code with your household so recipes, the grocery list, and the week’s plan stay in sync.
  • Meet FridgeGenie. A little mascot who shows up while the app is working, plus a few starter recipes so you can cook something tonight.

Free and Plus

Honey, Dinner? is free to download, with no ads and no account to create.

Free includes starter recipes, recipes you add yourself, the grocery list, household meal planning, and cook mode as text. You also get 3 TikTok or Instagram imports a week, 2 fridge or receipt scans a week, and one full cook with the voice and step pictures so you can try it.

Honey, Dinner? Plus is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial on both. Plus unlocks unlimited imports and scans, and the voice and step pictures in every cook. Family Sharing is on, so one subscription covers the household. You can manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.
