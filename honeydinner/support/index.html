FAQ

How do I save a recipe from TikTok or Instagram?

On your iPhone, open a public recipe video, tap Share, and choose Honey, Dinner? The app writes a recipe with ingredients and steps that you can edit before you save it.

Cook Now didn’t open the app

After an import, “Cook it now” should open Honey, Dinner? on that recipe. If the recipe saved but the app didn’t open, open Honey, Dinner? from your Home Screen — the recipe will be in your collection. You can also use “Finish in the app.” If this keeps happening, email us and tell us whether you shared from TikTok or Instagram.

How does fridge or receipt scanning work?

On the Scan screen, take a photo or upload one from your library. We’ll try to add what we see to your kitchen. Items from a receipt usually go to the pantry, and matching names can be checked off your grocery list. You can fix anything that was misread.

How do I share a kitchen with my household?

Create a kitchen in the app and share the code with the people you cook with. Anyone who joins sees the same recipes, grocery list, and meal plan. This is optional — you can use the app just on your own device. To stop sharing, leave the kitchen in the app.

What’s included for free, and what’s Plus?

Free includes starter recipes, recipes you add yourself, the grocery list, household meal planning, and text cook mode, plus 3 social imports a week, 2 scans a week, and one full cook with the voice and step pictures. Honey, Dinner? Plus ($7.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial) unlocks unlimited imports and scans, and the voice and step pictures in every cook. Family Sharing is on. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings (Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions).

The voice or listening isn’t working

Hands-free cook mode needs microphone and speech-recognition access. Open iOS Settings → Honey, Dinner? and allow both, then try cook mode again.

How do I delete my data?

Leave a shared kitchen in the app to stop syncing. To request deletion of a kitchen or other information we hold, email support@shakeandbakelabs.com. Uninstalling the app removes what is stored only on your device; it does not cancel a subscription. See our privacy policy.
