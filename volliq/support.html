FAQ

How do I get the best analysis?

Place the phone 8–12 feet away so the whole body is in view, use good light and a plain background, and keep the phone still.

Which skills does VOLLIQ coach?

Setting, passing, serving and hitting. Each rep gets a score and one or two simple tips to try next.

Can I analyze a video I already have?

Yes. In the camera screen, tap "Use a video" and pick a clip under 90 seconds.

Do my videos get uploaded?

No. Videos and body-movement data are analyzed and stored only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in. Your practices are saved only on this device.

How do I delete videos or all data?

Go to Profile. You can delete all videos while keeping results, choose to auto-delete videos after 7 or 30 days, or delete all data.

I got a new phone. Where did my history go?

History lives only on the device that recorded it and isn't backed up by the app, so it won't move to a new phone.

The AI coach says it's resting.

The friendly wording service has a usage limit. Your measured tips are still shown; try again later.

How do I turn off vibration?

Profile → Vibration → Off.
